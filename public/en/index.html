Our origins

It began when Enzo left to study in the United States.

His move to study at Snow College turned a family adventure into first-hand experience: understanding the process, assessing the costs and preparing for daily life there.

Faced with the choices, uncertainty and possibilities opened by this journey, one conviction emerged :

“Do not simply wait for opportunities to arise, but try to create them.”

Westward Co. grew from this experience and a desire to build differently.

The France / USA pillar brings together Campus, Digital and the Mon Enfant aux USA media platform. The Lifestyle pillar combines Coffee Shop and Cowboy Culture.
