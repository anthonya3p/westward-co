Our story

It all began with a family project.

When my son chose to continue his studies in the United States, what was initially meant to be a student adventure gradually became much more.

By experiencing the procedures, decisions, opportunities and challenges of a life project between France and the United States from the inside, one idea took root:

“Do not simply wait for opportunities to arise, but try to create them.”

Westward Co. grew from this reflection.

Not around one single activity, but around several projects developed with the same intention: to build, create and open new possibilities for my children’s future.

Over time, these projects have taken different forms. Today, Westward Co. is the identity that brings them together.

Our vision

Creating meaningful projects and giving them room to evolve.

Westward Co. was not built around a single business sector.

The projects within it may be very different, but they share the same origin: the desire to create new possibilities, build for the long term and avoid being limited to a single framework.

Some projects are developed in France, while others are focused on the United States. Some already exist, while others are still being built.

The goal is not to force everything into one activity, but to give each project its own identity, development and destination, while keeping Westward Co. as the common thread.

What comes next

Westward Co. will continue to evolve through the projects, encounters and opportunities that give it meaning.
