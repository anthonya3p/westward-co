Support

Understand the steps before making a decision.

Choosing to study in the United States raises many questions: the school, budget, application, visa, housing and arrival.

Westward Co. Campus offers personal, step-by-step support to help each family understand its options, organize the process and move forward with a clearer view of the project.

Admission and visa decisions are made exclusively by the relevant schools and authorities.

02

Our method

Four decisions to make, in the right order.

You do not receive an abstract checklist. Each step answers a concrete question, produces a clear result and prepares the next decision.

  1. 01Understand

    “Is my project realistic?”

    Start with the right assessment.

    Academic profile, English level, goals, schedule and budget: we review the essential elements from the start.

    OutcomeA clear view of the possibilities and points requiring attention.
  2. 02Choose

    “Which options truly suit me?”

    Compare without losing focus.

    We review schools, programs, admission requirements and overall cost to identify coherent choices.

    OutcomeA reasoned selection tailored to the family project.
  3. 03Prepare

    “What needs to be done, and when?”

    Turn the project into a schedule.

    Application, supporting documents, deadlines and visa: every step is organized clearly, without unrealistic promises.

    OutcomeA precise roadmap for moving forward with confidence.
  4. 04Leave

    “How can I prepare for arrival?”

    Prepare for everyday life.

    Travel, housing, phone, banking and first points of reference: we prepare the practical matters that count once in the United States.

    OutcomeA better-prepared departure for both the student and the family.

Real-life experience

Born from a real family journey.

Westward Co. Campus grew out of Anthony’s experience as the father of a French student who went to study in the United States.

The research, procedures, financial decisions, move and daily life revealed the questions families truly ask—and the information they often lack at the start.

The goal is simple: share this experience and provide an accessible point of contact throughout the preparation.

A transparent approach

Clear terms before any step is taken.

Depending on the chosen school and an active partnership, support through departure may be covered by the partner school.

Any fees, compensation paid by a school and the exact scope of support will always be clearly presented to the family before any commitment.

Frequently asked questions

Initial answers before we speak.

When should the process begin?

Ideally, several months before the desired intake. The right timeline depends on the school, the application and the time required for formalities.

Is support always free for families?

Not necessarily. When an active partnership allows the school to cover support, this is clearly explained. In other situations, any fees are announced before the process begins.

Does Westward Co. Campus guarantee admission or a visa?

No. Admission decisions belong to each school, and visas are issued exclusively by U.S. authorities. Support helps families understand and prepare for the journey.

Can you help with settling in the United States?

Travel, housing, phone and initial practical needs can be discussed during preparation. Any available services are specified according to the project and destination.
